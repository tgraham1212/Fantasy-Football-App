Every remaining week is simulated 10,000 times using each team's current roster, the real schedule, Sleeper's player projections and how far real scores have strayed from projections in the past. The playoffs and losers bracket are played out with the league's actual rules. These are chances, not predictions: a team at 70% misses roughly three times in ten.
