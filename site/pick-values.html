What a Pick Is Worth

Every dynasty rookie pick is valued by the WAR its Players have produced over the eight seasons after their draft, wherever they played. Picks are grouped by round and by third of the round: early (picks 1 to 4), middle (5 to 8) and late (9 to 12). A pick not used yet is worth its group's value; the Trades tab uses these values.

Measured: the average of every pick in the group. Model value: the same, with any group worth more than the group before it averaged with it, so an earlier pick is never worth less than a later one. The range is where the group's average falls 80% of the time when the picks are resampled; single picks vary far more.

WAR in Each Season

The same picks by round, one season at a time. First-round Players add the most in their third season and little by their eighth. Second- and third-round Players score below replacement level as rookies and rarely make it back.

Value by Draft Slot

The average pick at each slot through three seasons and through eight. Single slots swing with one Player, which is why the values are set by thirds of the round instead.

How the Values Are Made

  1. Rookie order. The 2013 to 2021 classes are ranked by each rookie's average pick in Dynasty League Football startup mock drafts held from May to August, after the NFL draft, as collected by mizelle.net. From 2022 the classes are this league's own rookie drafts. Where both exist, the two orders agree closely.
  2. WAR. Every season uses this site's Player WAR with the dynasty league's scoring and lineup, over weeks 1 to 14. A season a Player did not play counts zero.
  3. Eight seasons. A group's value adds up the average WAR of its picks in each of the eight seasons after the draft, using every class that has reached that season.
  4. Picks not used yet. A pick whose draft order is set takes its group's value. A pick in the next draft takes the third of the round its team's place in the current Forecast gives it, worst finish first. A pick further away takes its round's average.
  5. Updates. The values are rebuilt once a year, after a season ends, as each class adds a season.
