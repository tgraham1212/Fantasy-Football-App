SITE COPY FOR EDITING
=====================

Edit the text under each TEXT: line, save, and tell Claude; the tests flag the edits and Claude
checks with you before applying them to the site (python -m ffapp site-copy --apply).
- Leave the [ID] and WHERE lines alone; they tell the tool where each piece goes.
- Anything inside ${...} is filled in by the page (a week number, a name, a date). Keep it, move it
  within the sentence if you like, but do not change what is inside the braces.
- Inline tags such as <em>, <strong>, <code> or <a ...> are written back as you leave them.
- To ask for a piece to be deleted, leave its TEXT empty and say so in a NOTE: line.
- Add a NOTE: line under any entry to explain an edit or ask a question; notes are kept.
- Entries marked (generated) are built by the page; some are sentences that join others
  ("add-on"), so read them together.

[WAR-03] list item

TEXT:
<strong>Start percentage.</strong> ESPN publishes the share of its leagues starting each player. Seasons up to 2026 have one frozen value per player; from 2027 the value is captured every week.

[WAR-04] list item
WHERE: site/index.html line 183
TEXT:
<strong>Consensus lineup.</strong> Each week the most-started players with a game fill every position's starting slots for 12 teams, then the most-started leftover RB, WR and TE fill the flex.

[WAR-05] list item
WHERE: site/index.html line 184
TEXT:
<strong>Slot means and spread.</strong> Over the season, each slot's average points and variance from those lineups. The opponent is a normal distribution: mean = sum of slot means, spread = square root of the summed variances.

[WAR-06] list item
WHERE: site/index.html line 185
TEXT:
<strong>Replacement cut.</strong> The three most-started players past each position's cut are that week's replacement players; their season average is the replacement level.

[WAR-07] list item
WHERE: site/index.html line 186
TEXT:
<strong>Weekly win probability.</strong> A player's week is their actual points in a lineup whose other slots sit at the slot means, so the win probability is the share of the opponent distribution below the league mean plus (score − slot mean). A week with no game played takes the replacement level; a game played for zero counts as zero.

[WAR-08] list item
WHERE: site/index.html line 187
TEXT:
<strong>Expected wins and WAR.</strong> Win probabilities sum over regular-season weeks 1 to 14. WAR subtracts what a replacement-level player would earn every week, so a replacement season is exactly 0.
