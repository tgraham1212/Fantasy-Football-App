# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Calculation explained
The page SHALL end with an explanation of the calculation: a flow chart from start percentage to consensus lineup, slot means and spread, replacement cut, weekly win probability, season sum and the replacement baseline, followed by a week-by-week walkthrough of Kittle and Bowers in 2024 using the selected league's own weekly scores, win probabilities and totals. Weeks a player did not play SHALL read "Bye" or "Out" with no score or win probability. When the league has no 2024 file the walkthrough SHALL be omitted and the flow chart kept.

#### Scenario: Walkthrough numbers
- **WHEN** the redraft page is shown
- **THEN** the walkthrough lists each 2024 week for both players with the score and its win probability for weeks played, marks Kittle's missed weeks as Bye or Out, and ends with both expected-wins totals and WARs
